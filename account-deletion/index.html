How to request account deletion

To request deletion of your Qafila account and associated personal data we hold for the app, send an email from the same email address registered on your Qafila account to:

infonexgenztech@gmail.com

Use subject line: Qafila account deletion request

Your email must include:

  • Your Qafila account email address (if different from the sending address, include both and explain the relationship)
  • A clear statement that you want permanent deletion of your Qafila account and associated app data

We may contact you to verify your identity before we process the request (for example, by replying from the registered email or asking for information only the account holder would know).

In-app deletion (not currently available)

A self-service Delete account option in the app and fully automated backend deletion have not been verified as implemented in the current release. Do not rely on an in-app button unless a future app update explicitly provides one that we confirm is live. Until then, use the email process above.

Account and profile information involved

Your request relates to data tied to your Qafila account, which may include:

  • Your Firebase Authentication account (email sign-in or linked sign-in method)
  • Your profile in our database (such as name, email, profile photo URL, location sharing preference, and push notification token)
  • Your membership in a family group (group code, admin role if applicable, and your entry on the group member list)

What we aim to delete or anonymize

After we verify your request, we aim to delete or anonymize personal data that we no longer need to retain, including:

Data categoryIntended action
Firebase Authentication accountDelete after verification, where our systems support it

Completion may depend on server-side deletion capability in the live service. Automated end-to-end deletion has not been verified as available in the current release.

Family records, SOS/Lost alerts, and location data

Some data may require separate handling or retention review:

  • Family groups: Deleting your account removes you from your group’s member list. Other members’ accounts and data are not deleted. If you are the only administrator of a group, we may need to resolve group administration (for example, transfer admin or adjust the group) before your account can be fully removed. Contact us if this applies to you.
  • Location history: Location stored with your profile or past updates may be deleted with your account, but copies may remain for a time in backups, logs, or disaster-recovery systems until those cycles complete.
  • SOS and Lost Mode records: Alert records (identity, group, location, timestamps) may be retained only as long as needed for alert delivery, safety follow-up, troubleshooting, or legal and security requirements, then deleted or anonymized when no longer needed.

What may be retained

We delete or anonymize data when it is no longer needed for the purposes for which it was collected, subject to:

  • Applicable law, regulation, or valid legal requests
  • Security, fraud prevention, and abuse investigation
  • Operational backups and logs that are overwritten on a rolling basis
  • Information that must be kept because other users or family members’ legitimate use of the service depends on non-personal or aggregated records

We do not promise that every copy of data disappears instantly from all systems.

Processing your request

We will process verified deletion requests as reasonably practicable. We do not guarantee deletion within a specific number of days. Complex requests (family admin resolution, incomplete verification, or technical limitations) may take longer. We will inform you if we cannot fulfill part of a request and explain why, where appropriate.
