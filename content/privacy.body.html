2. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Qafila (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use our mobile application for Hajj/Umrah companion features, including family groups, maps, location sharing, SOS, and Lost Mode.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Account and profile

When you create and use an account, we may collect and store:

  • Name — to display your profile and identify you to your family group.
  • Email address — to create and manage your account, sign in, and support account recovery.
  • Account identifiers — assigned by Google Firebase Authentication to secure your session and link your profile to our database.
  • Profile photo — if you sign in with Google or provide a photo, we may store a profile image URL linked to your account.

3.2 Family group data

To connect you with your family group, we may process:

  • Family group code and membership in a group.
  • Admin status, where applicable, for group administration.
  • Information visible to other members of your group — such as your name, sharing status, and location when sharing is enabled — according to our access rules in the app backend.

This information is shared only with members of your family group, not with the general public.

3.3 Location data (foreground)

When you use map, family, SOS, or Lost Mode features, we may collect precise location (such as latitude, longitude, and related technical fields) and store it with your profile or alert records.

  • Location sharing preference — you can turn location sharing on or off in the app (for example, Profile → Location), where that control is available in your version of the app.
  • Google Maps — map display and place search may send location or search queries to Google Maps Platform when you use those features.

3.4 Background location

Qafila is designed so that background location (when the app is not visible on screen) is used only when you have explicitly enabled location sharing in the app and granted the location permissions your device requires (including background location permission on Android, where applicable).

  • Purpose: To help authorized members of your family group see your location on the family map and to support safety features during Hajj/Umrah travel. We do not use location for advertising. Location is not shared with the general public.
  • Opt-in and permissions: Background location should not be used unless you turn on sharing and accept the relevant system permission prompts. Permission wording and steps vary by Android version and device manufacturer.
  • What to expect: Location updates may be periodic, not continuous or real-time. Updates can be delayed or stopped by your device (battery optimization, force-stop, loss of GPS signal, network issues, or Android background execution limits).
  • Your controls: You can disable location sharing in the app, revoke location permissions in your device Settings, or log out. These actions are intended to stop new location updates. They may not immediately delete location data already stored with your account or in backups.
  • Implementation note: The product approach described above reflects our intended design. We have not independently verified that every control, disclosure, and permission flow behaves exactly as described in all app versions currently in development or testing.

3.5 SOS and Lost Mode

When you activate SOS or Lost Mode, we collect alert information needed to notify your family group, which may include your identity, family group association, location at the time of the alert, and timestamps related to the alert.

3.6 Notifications

We store a push notification token (FCM token) on your account record so we can send family alert notifications. Whether you receive notifications also depends on your device settings and Firebase Cloud Messaging.

3.7 Local storage on your device

We may store preferences on your device (such as onboarding completion) using local storage on the device.

3.8 Analytics and usage data

Based on our review of the app codebase, we did not identify a third-party analytics SDK (such as Google Analytics for Firebase) in the reviewed version. We do not describe analytics tracking as active in this policy. If we introduce analytics or similar tools in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, provide any additional notice or choices.

Our in-app terms or other screens may mention device or usage information in general terms; those references should be read together with this policy.

4. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Provide and secure your account
  • Operate family groups and related dashboards
  • Show maps and optional place search
  • Share location with your family group when you enable sharing
  • Send SOS/Lost alerts and related notifications
  • Protect the service through authentication and database access rules

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use advertising SDKs identified in our app audit.

6. Sharing with service providers

We use service providers to operate Qafila, including:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, Cloud Firestore database, Cloud Functions, and Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • Google Maps Platform — map display and place-related features.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies. You can review Google’s privacy information at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

We do not share your location with the public or with users outside your family group except as described in this policy or as required by law.

7. Data security

We use measures intended to protect information, such as encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for data in transit and access rules designed to limit who can read family-related data in our database. No method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy, including operating the app, maintaining safety features, resolving disputes, enforcing our terms, and meeting applicable legal, security, and operational requirements.

  • Account and profile data — kept while your account is active and for a limited period afterward as needed for deletion processing, backups, or legal obligations.
  • Location data — kept only as long as needed for the location-sharing and safety purposes described above. Turning off sharing or logging out is intended to stop new updates; previously stored location may remain until deleted through account deletion or our retention processes. Copies may persist for a time in backups or logs.
  • SOS and Lost Mode records — kept only as long as needed for alert delivery, family safety, troubleshooting, and similar operational purposes, then deleted or anonymized when no longer needed, subject to the same backup and legal limitations.

We do not state fixed retention periods in days or months in this policy because retention depends on how you use the app, technical backup cycles, and applicable requirements.

Depending on your device and app version, you may be able to:

  • Log out of your account
  • Manage notification and location permissions in device Settings

10. Account and data deletion

Google Play requires a way to request account and data deletion for apps that create accounts.

  1. Email: Send a request to infonexgenztech@gmail.com with subject line Qafila account deletion request. Include the email address registered on your Qafila account and a clear statement that you want your account and associated app data deleted.
  2. Web: See our account deletion page for the same process and additional detail.

We may need to verify your identity before processing a request. We will delete or anonymize data that we no longer need to retain, subject to applicable legal, security, and operational requirements (including limited backup retention). We do not promise completion within a specific number of days.

In-app deletion: A self-service delete-account flow in the app and fully automated backend cleanup have not been verified as implemented in the current release. Until they are available and verified, use the email process above.
